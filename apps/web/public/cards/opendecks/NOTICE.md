# OpenDecks playing cards

The SVG card faces and card back in this directory come from
[OpenDecks Public Domain and CC0 Playing Cards](https://github.com/AustinGabriel/OpenDecks-Public-Domain-and-CC0-Playing-Cards).

They are distributed under CC0 1.0. The upstream license is preserved in
`LICENSE.txt`.

The face cards (J, Q, K) and the card back are served as WebP rasterisations (330×462) of those
SVGs for regular DOM cards. The `hires/` J, Q, and K WebPs are 660×924 rasterisations of the same
originals for 3D tables. The vector originals were 300–700 KB each for a card drawn about 100 px
wide; they remain in git history and in the upstream repository.
